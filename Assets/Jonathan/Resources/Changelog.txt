# The Revival Update | November 2026
## New
- Hex pillar seas: every match is a fresh board with terrain rising out of the water
- Cloud fog of war that parts around the hex you aim at
- Bots come in Easy, Medium and Hard
- New specials, each with its own shape: air strikes, barrages, torpedoes, sonar sweeps, harpoons and depth charges
- Number keys for ships, weapons and patterns, and every key can be rebound
- Captains to unlock and take into matches
- Pick your color and the enemy's
- Both boards side by side on the match report
## Improved
- All new menus, from the title screen to game over
- Top and pivot views that follow every volley
- Hit and miss markers, splashes and shockwaves
- A glowing sun, side colored skies and endless water
## Fixed
- Matches that wouldn't start on a fresh install
- Bots firing at the same miss twice
